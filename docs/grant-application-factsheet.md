# Grant Application Factsheet

Date: April 2026
Project: LughaKonnect
Site: https://kamusi-yetu.vercel.app/

## One-Line Description

LughaKonnect is a community-built dictionary and language infrastructure platform for Kenyan languages, designed to preserve underrepresented languages and build the lexical and phrase data needed for better translation systems.

## Problem

Many Kenyan languages are digitally underrepresented.
That creates a structural disadvantage in search, education, civic access, language technology, and AI systems.
If these languages are absent from machine-readable dictionaries, phrase banks, and reviewable translation data, they will remain absent from the systems shaping the future.

## What Exists Today

LughaKonnect already supports:

- multilingual dictionary entries across active Kenyan languages
- contribution and moderation workflows
- bridge-based translation candidates via English and Swahili
- language discovery and progress surfaces
- data ingestion and rollback workflows for lexicon growth

## Current Product Position

LughaKonnect is currently in the dictionary and phrase foundation stage.

What works today:

- verified lexical entries
- searchable dictionary flows
- bridge-assisted translation candidate generation
- contributor submission workflows
- moderation and language health tracking

What is still in progress:

- deeper phrase coverage
- stronger usage-example coverage
- better contextual translation quality
- sentence-level translation capability

## Why This Matters

This project is not only about preservation.
It is about building practical language infrastructure that can support learning, multilingual communication, safer access to information, and future translation systems that reflect how people actually speak.

## Why This Founder

The project is founder-led by Eddie Ezekiel Ochieng, who is building both the product layer and the underlying data and moderation systems required to make the platform credible over time.

## What Funding Would Accelerate

Funding would accelerate the next practical milestone:

- expanding phrase and usage-example coverage
- improving moderation and quality-control tooling
- increasing contributor throughput for underrepresented languages
- strengthening the public product and reviewer-facing proof of progress

## Near-Term Milestone

The next milestone is to make LughaKonnect clearly usable as public language infrastructure rather than only a promising prototype.
That means tighter trust signals, visible metrics, richer phrase/context coverage, and stronger public proof of quality controls.

## Reviewer Takeaway

LughaKonnect should be evaluated as foundational language infrastructure for Kenyan languages: a technically serious, culturally important project that is early, real, and capable of compounding with focused support.
